New Employee Onboarding Guide

Week 1: IT Setup and Orientation
New joiners receive their laptop, official email ID, and access credentials on Day 1. A mandatory orientation session covering company history, values, and code of conduct is held every Monday. Employees must complete the mandatory compliance training modules on the Learning Management System (LMS) within the first 5 working days.

Week 2: Team Integration
Each new employee is assigned a buddy from their team to help them navigate day-to-day processes. Managers are expected to schedule a 1:1 with new joiners within the first two weeks to set expectations and initial goals.

Probation Period
The standard probation period for new full-time employees is 6 months. A formal performance review is conducted at the end of the probation period, based on which employment is confirmed. Probation may be extended by up to 3 months at the manager's discretion if performance benchmarks are not met.

Benefits Enrollment
Employees must enroll in group health insurance and other benefit programs within 15 days of joining through the HR benefits portal. Failure to enroll within this window means employees must wait until the next open enrollment period.
