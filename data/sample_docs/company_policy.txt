Employee Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid leave per calendar year, which includes casual and sick leave combined. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Sick leave taken due to unforeseen illness can be applied retroactively within 2 working days of returning to office.

Unused leave up to a maximum of 10 days can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Any leave beyond this cap will lapse at the end of the year and will not be encashed.

Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 8 days per month, subject to manager approval. Employees working from home are expected to be available on official communication channels during standard working hours (9:30 AM to 6:30 PM). Any exceptions to the 8-day limit require written approval from the department head.

Reimbursement Policy

Travel and client-meeting related expenses are reimbursable upon submission of valid bills within 30 days of the expense. Internet and mobile reimbursement for employees working remotely is capped at INR 1500 per month and requires a monthly bill submission through the finance portal.
